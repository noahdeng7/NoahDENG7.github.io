Research Experience

National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA)

Working with Dr. Angela Lyons' group at the NCSA this past summer, I worked to use machine learning to model internal human migration in developing countries. I am still working with her team today, hoping to use these models to uncover the causes of human migration in Brazil.

LiuLab

Working with Dr. Liu and his group at Tufts, we are working on evaluating physics-based subseasonal-to-seasonal (S2S) weather forecasting models' ability to forecast extreme events like hurricanes, heatwaves, etc.

Independent Research

I am currently working on a project involving one of my biggest interests in ML: sparsity! By using dynamic sparse training (DST) we can maintain the same performance in accuracy with less than 1% of the connections! I am currently working to study the emergent behavior of the sparse topologies that are produced by various DST methods.

Projects

Supamines

SUPAMINES (the Self-SUPervised-Attention-based MINo inferencE System) is one of my ML passion projects. Utilizing PPO on a transformer and a lagrangian constraint, this model uses the transformer architecture and novel reward system to match the current best model, BetaTetris, with only a quarter of the parameters! You can download and watch the model play for yourself, or view the demo here.
